Terms of Use

Last updated October 3, 2026

These are starter terms in plain language. A full version is on the way; until then, this is how the site works and what we ask of you.

What this is

Spokane Tech Jobs is a free directory of startups, tech employers, job openings, people and community spaces across Spokane, Eastern Washington and North Idaho.

Using the site

You can browse without an account. An account lets you save jobs, keep a profile, add a company, claim a listing and post roles. Be accurate, be kind, and use the site for its purpose: connecting people with work and with each other here.

Listings and job posts

  • Adding a company and posting jobs are free.
  • New companies, claims and job posts are reviewed by a person before they go live, and we may decline or remove anything that's inaccurate, misleading, or not a real opening.
  • Job posts include a pay range and run for 90 days, and the poster can refresh them for another 90.
  • You may only post jobs for a company you've claimed, and only claim a company you work for.

Profiles

Some profiles are listed from public information before the person joins. If one is about you, you can claim it with the email on file, edit it, or take it down. Your profile is yours.

The open data

The directory's company data is published as open data under CC BY 4.0. You're welcome to reuse it with attribution to Spokane Tech Jobs. The site's code is MIT-licensed.

No warranty

We work to keep everything accurate and sourced, but the site is provided as is. Check details with the employer before you rely on them.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms as the site grows, and the date above will change when we do. Questions: hello@spokanetechjobs.com.

Innovation Collective

Builds innovation economies for cities

Builds innovation economies for cities. It works across economic development, community development, and real estate and place (innovation districts, downtown revitalization), and runs Build_, its AI community operating system. Build_ has two parts: Build Cities, a live map of a city's projects, events and people, and UdayOS, an AI guide trained on each city's own data. Build_ says it is active in 62 cities on six continents. Coeur d'Alene is the headquarters and the model city.
